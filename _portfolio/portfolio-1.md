## Project Description

Automobile fuel efficiency is an important consideration for consumers, automotive manufacturers, and industry analysts when evaluating vehicle performance and energy consumption. However, understanding how different vehicle characteristics relate to fuel efficiency can be challenging when examining raw automotive data. This project addresses that problem by developing an interactive narrative visualization using a 2017 automobile dataset to explore relationships among engine cylinders, city and highway miles per gallon (MPG), and fuel types. The goal was to make automotive efficiency data more accessible and understandable through visual storytelling.

Using **D3.js, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS**, I developed an interactive three-scene visualization that guides users through key aspects of automobile efficiency. The first scene examines the relationship between engine cylinders and average highway MPG, the second compares city and highway fuel efficiency, and the third explores average efficiency across different fuel types. I cleaned and prepared the dataset, performed data parsing and aggregation, and incorporated interactive scatterplots, bar charts, annotations, hover tooltips, and fuel-type filtering. These features allow users to explore patterns and compare vehicle characteristics interactively.

The primary stakeholders for this project are **vehicle consumers, automotive industry analysts, and manufacturers** who may benefit from understanding fuel efficiency trends. The visualization supports consumer decisions about which vehicle characteristics to prioritize when considering fuel economy, while helping analysts and manufacturers explore patterns across engine configurations and fuel categories. By transforming raw automobile data into an interactive narrative, the project demonstrates how data visualization can communicate complex relationships, support informed comparisons, and provide insights into vehicle efficiency without requiring users to interpret raw datasets.

**Technologies:** D3.js, JavaScript, HTML, CSS
