# About me

Hi! I'm Charlene Khun, a Computer Science graduate from San Jose State University and currently pursuing my Master's in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC).

I'm an aspiring **Data Analyst and Data Scientist** passionate about transforming complex datasets into meaningful insights that support data-driven decision-making. My technical skills include **Python, SQL, R, Pandas, scikit-learn, Tableau, and D3.js**, with experience in data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, statistical analysis, machine learning, and interactive data visualization.

Through my academic and personal projects, I've gained hands-on experience analyzing San Francisco rental housing data, preparing large historical datasets for analysis, developing interactive dashboards, and applying machine learning techniques to real-world problems.

I'm currently **seeking entry-level Data Analyst and Data Scientist opportunities** where I can apply my analytical skills, solve business problems, and continue growing as a data professional.
